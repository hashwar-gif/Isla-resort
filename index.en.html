Our story

More than a hotel, a family legacy

For over twenty years, La Isla Resort has been a cherished family legacy in the heart of the Amazon rainforest. Surrounded by breathtaking nature, we offer comfortable rooms, outstanding service and a range of activities to make your stay unforgettable.
